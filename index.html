EV Car Dashboard

Dashboard · Data Visualization

An electric vehicle dashboard showing battery percentage, charging status, estimated range and driving efficiency. It uses simulated data to recreate the behaviour of a vehicle in use.

What I built

  • Live gauge updates driven by a JavaScript simulation loop.
  • A range-over-time chart drawn by hand using inline SVG.
  • Four KPI tiles showing battery percentage, charging status, estimated range and efficiency.
  • A layout tested at 200% browser zoom and on a 360px viewport.
